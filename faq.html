Buying and files

What am I buying? Is a physical product shipped?

No. Every product is a digital 3D print file; nothing is shipped. You print the file yourself or have it printed by a print service.

Which file formats do I get?

Every design comes print-ready in four formats: STL, OBJ, 3MF and GLB. Large models are split into parts where the size needs it.

How do I order and receive the files?

Add the designs you like to your cart and send your request with your email address; we reply by email within 24 hours. Once your payment is confirmed, the design files are sent to your email address, ready to download and use. Keep a backup copy of your files.

How do I pay?

Prices are in US dollars. You can pay through Payoneer or by bank transfer to our US bank account; we send the payment details by email when we reply to your request. Taxes may be added depending on your location, and your bank may charge a currency conversion fee.

Can I get a refund?

Digital files cannot be returned once downloaded. But if a file is broken, the wrong file arrived, it is significantly not as described, or you were charged twice, tell us within 14 days: we first fix or replace the file, and if we cannot, you get a full refund. See the Refund Policy for details.

License

Can I sell the prints I make?

Yes. You may sell physical objects you print from the file, or have printed for you by a print service. Painting, hand-finishing and assembling them is covered too. To advertise your prints, use photos of your own prints, not the images from this website.

Can I share or resell the files?

No. The digital files, and any modified version of them, may not be shared, sold or uploaded anywhere. Mass production such as injection molding, casting from molds or large production runs needs a separate written license. The full terms are in the File License.

Printing

The model is larger than my print bed. What should I do?

Wall reliefs, mirrors and floor lamps are modeled at real size (most reliefs are around 600 mm wide, floor lamps 1 m tall), so they print in sections. The technical notes on each product page explain where to split the model, with seams placed where they hide in the design. Contact us if you need a custom split for your printer.

How do I join the sections?

Sections are joined with pins and glue. For slim parts such as the thin legs and long necks of some floor lamps, the technical notes recommend reinforcing with steel tubes. Check the notes of each product; they list the seams and the points to reinforce.

Can I change the size of a model?

Yes. Figurines and vases are modeled at a reference size and scale freely; scale uniformly to keep the balance. When scaling down, watch the thickness of fine details: the technical notes give the minimum thickness for each model.

How accurate are the filament and print time figures?

They are rough estimates at the listed size (1.2 mm walls, 15% infill). Your slicer, printer and settings will give different figures; they are guidance, not guarantees.

Reliefs, mirrors, lamps and vases

How do I hang a wall relief or mirror?

Some models have built-in keyhole slots on the back; the print specs on the product page list them under "Hanging". For the others, add keyhole hangers, a French cleat or a wall-mount bracket. In every case, use screws and hangers rated for the finished weight.

Is the mirror glass included?

No, you are buying the print file of the frame. Have the glass cut locally: measure the opening on your printed frame, order 3–4 mm mirror glass and fix it with mirror adhesive or clips. Where the glass size is fixed, it is listed on the product page.

Which bulb should I use in the floor lamps? Is it safe?

Use low-heat LED bulbs or LED strips only; never use hot bulbs inside plastic parts. Wire the lamp with a certified lamp cord set and switch, and have it installed by a qualified person where required. Weight the base so it cannot tip, and leave a cable channel of at least 8 mm.

Can I put water in the vases?

As printed, the vases are decorative dry vases. For fresh flowers, print in PETG with solid walls and seal the inside, or place a glass tube or glass insert inside. Always test for leaks before arranging flowers. Printed objects are not food-safe.

How do I get the color and finish shown? Can I ask for a different color or texture?

The product images show each design in one suggested color scheme and finish. The technical notes of each product include a finishing recipe for that look (for example primer, dry-brush, dark wash and clear coat), so you can reproduce it or choose your own colors. Custom color, texture, size or split versions of a file are quoted separately; just contact us.
