About

Decorans Design is a 3D design studio creating sculptural home décor for 3D printing. Every piece is developed as a full print-ready brief covering wall thickness, layer height, support strategy and material selection, rather than a rendered concept alone.

Load-bearing geometry is modeled parametrically in SolidWorks and Fusion 360, while Blender, ZBrush and Cinema 4D handle the organic, sculptural side of the catalogue. Every design is treated as an engineering problem first: the geometry has to make physical and structural sense before it becomes a printable file.
