About

Hilal Polat is a Mechanical & Industrial & 3D Designer based in Türkiye, working at the intersection of classical industrial design and applied mechanical engineering. The practice is centered on sculptural 3D-print design, where each home-decoration piece is developed as a full print-ready brief covering wall thickness, layer height, support strategy, and material selection, rather than a rendered concept alone.

CAD work runs through SolidWorks and Fusion 360 for parametric, tolerance-driven modeling of load-bearing geometry, while Blender, ZBrush, and Cinema 4D handle the organic, sculptural, and rendering side of the print catalogue. Every project is treated as an engineering problem first: the geometry has to make physical and structural sense before it becomes a finished drawing or a printable file.
